# Product-wide

Rules that apply to more than one feature.

## Requirements

- P1 Every user signs in via SSO through the platform identity provider. \[org default\] Applies to: all.
- P2 Overdue-task reminders and escalations are sent by email. Applies to: F3.
- P3 New hires are entered manually by the HR Coordinator; there is no integration with an external HR system. Applies to: F1.

## Decisions

- No multi-currency, payroll or benefits data is tracked — this product is
limited to onboarding task coordination.
